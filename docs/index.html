🚀 Cosmo's Space Trip

A Solar System learning game for kids aged 6–9.

Explore all 8 planets with Cosmo the friendly alien, each with its own hands-on mission: spin Earth, drive a rover on Mars, photograph Jupiter's moons, hop Saturn's rings, scan Venus with radar, race the sunrise on Mercury, slingshot around Uranus and ride Neptune's winds. Dress Cosmo up with the stars you earn! No ads, no accounts, no data collection, and it works offline.

Common questions

How do I open Settings? Tap the gear on the map, then ask a grown-up to answer the multiplication question.

There's no sound. Check the ringer/silent switch and the volume. Sound, music and read-aloud can each be turned on or off in Settings.

How do I start again? Settings → Start over (tap it twice to confirm).
