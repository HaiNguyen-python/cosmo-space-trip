## Description (4000)
Join Cosmo, a friendly little alien, on a trip across our Solar System!

Cosmo's Space Trip helps children aged 6 to 9 learn about space by doing, not just reading. Every planet is a hands-on mission that shows a real science idea:

🌍 EARTH: spin Earth to give the rooster a morning and the owl a night.
🔴 MARS: drive a rover and float over boulders in the weak gravity.
🪐 JUPITER: photograph its big moons and the Great Red Spot.
💫 SATURN: hop across the real rings on drifting chunks of ice.
📡 VENUS: use radar to see through the thick clouds.
🔥 MERCURY: walk with the slow sunrise between scorching day and freezing night.
🎳 URANUS: fling a probe and let gravity bend its path.
💙 NEPTUNE: ride the fastest winds in the Solar System.

BONUS MISSIONS
🌙 Make the Moon's phases • 🪨 Sort asteroids with a tractor beam • ☄️ Ride a comet and catch a meteor shower • ✨ Watch the life story of a star • 🛰️ Float aboard the Space Station • 🌊 Dive under Europa's ice • 🚁 Fly a drone on Titan • 🌌 Travel to the black hole in the middle of the Milky Way

🎓 EXPERT MISSIONS
Every planet has a harder version for older explorers, with brand-new facts and a gold Expert badge.

🎤 COSMO'S STAGE
• Mission Control, a space quiz show about the places you've explored. Play alone, or as a family in two teams. Every team wins a silly prize!
• Space Camp: a 3-minute daily routine with a real astronaut exercise, a brain question and tonight's real Moon. Collect camp patches (no streaks, no pressure).
• Two sing-alongs: the Planet Song and the sleepy Moon Song, with music made right on the device.

🏗️ BUILD AND PLAY
• Build a Mars Base and learn what astronauts need: air, water, food, power, a home and a radio. Then look at Mars rocks in the science lab and send a rocket home.
• Build your own rocket in the Rocket Workshop. Cosmo then flies YOUR rocket everywhere!
• Fling planets into orbit and crash rocks into the Moon in the Space Playground.
• Swipe a planet on the map to spin it and see its own (true!) trick.

🏠 COSMO'S WORLD
• Decorate Cosmo's Space Base with stickers, and see a new surprise every day.
• Grow space lettuce, zinnias and tomatoes in the greenhouse.
• Find 16 shy critters hiding in the missions.
• Join up 11 real constellations through Cosmo's telescope.
• Draw your discoveries in the Space Journal, with a real "Tonight's Moon" page.
• Read postcards from Cosmo's family and help him write back.

⭐ MORE TO EXPLORE
• Earn up to 3 stars per mission, and replay to beat your best
• Dress up Cosmo with hats and rocket paint jobs
• Collect 17 shiny fact cards
• Visit all 8 planets for the Grand Tour, and a secret at the edge of the Solar System...

MADE FOR EVERY YOUNG EXPLORER
• Everything Cosmo says is read aloud, perfect for early readers
• No timers, no lives, no "game over": kids can't fail, they just keep exploring
• Little Explorer mode makes every mission gentler for younger kids
• Calm effects mode turns down flashing, shaking and confetti
• Short, accurate facts kids love to repeat at the dinner table

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
• No data collection: nothing leaves the device
• Works completely offline
• Grown-up settings are behind a parental gate
• A "What your child learned" page for parents lists missions, facts, things to try together and this week's real sky

## App Review information
- **Sign-in required:** No
- **Notes for the reviewer:**
  > Cosmo's Space Trip is an offline educational game for children aged 6–8. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, network requests, external links or purchases. The Settings screen (gear icon on the map) is behind a parental gate: answer the multiplication question shown, using the keypad. The "What your child learned" page is inside Settings and is built on the device from saved progress. Progress is stored only on the device.
