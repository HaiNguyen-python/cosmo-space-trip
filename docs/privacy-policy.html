Privacy Policy

Cosmo's Space Trip · Last updated 13 October 2026

In short: Cosmo's Space Trip does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and the app never connects to the internet.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Information stored on the device

To remember a child's progress (which planets have been visited, quiz stars, collected fact cards, and sound settings), the app saves a small amount of game data only on the device itself. This data is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it. The "Start over" option in Settings also erases it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social media features, and it does not link to external websites.

Read-aloud voice

Spoken text uses the speech voice built into the device's operating system. It works on the device without sending the text to us.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children aged 6–9 and follows Apple's Kids Category rules. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for parents to review or delete beyond the on-device game progress described above. The Settings area is protected by a parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
