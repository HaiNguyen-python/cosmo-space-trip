# Cosmo's Space Trip: Design Brief

An iPad-first learning game for **ages 6–9 (English)**. The player flies around the Solar System with **Cosmo**, a friendly little alien. At each planet they play a short mini-game, answer a gentle 2-question quiz, and collect a **fact card** for their Space Album.

## Pillars
1. **Show, don't tell.** Every planet's mini-game *is* its main fact. You learn that Earth's spin makes day and night by spinning Earth yourself. You learn about Mars's weak gravity by feeling the rover float.
2. **No failure.** No lives, no timers, no game-over. Wrong quiz answers get "try another one!" Missed samples just mean a smaller count.
3. **Read-aloud everywhere.** Every line Cosmo says is spoken (Web Speech API) and has a 🔊 replay button, for kids who are still learning to read.
4. **Facts a kid can repeat at dinner.** One short sentence each, checked for accuracy.
5. **Safe for kids.** No ads, accounts, chat or analytics. Progress stays on the device (Apple Kids Category friendly).

## Core loop
Map → tap planet → rocket flight → Cosmo intro → mini-game (2–4 min) → wrap-up facts → quiz (⭐ for a right answer on the first try) → fact card → map (next planet unlocks)

## Planned mini-games for the remaining planets
- **Mercury: Shadow Hop.** Dash between shadows. The sunny side is scorching and the shadows are freezing. Teaches that there's no air to hold heat.
- **Venus: Cloud Radar.** Fly through thick clouds by "radar" pings. Teaches about the greenhouse effect and the backwards spin.
- **Jupiter: Storm Surfer.** Ride the bands and steer around the Great Red Spot. Teaches size (Earth fits inside the spot).
- **Saturn: Ring Hopper.** Hop across ice chunks in the rings. A bathtub float gag teaches density.
- **Uranus: Roll Along.** Roll a sideways-tilted planet around the Sun. Teaches axial tilt.
- **Neptune: Wind Race.** Sail on the fastest winds in the Solar System. Teaches distance (a 165-year orbit).
- **Bonus:** the Moon (phases), the asteroid belt, Pluto and the dwarf planets, and the life of a star.

## Tech
Plain HTML5 Canvas + JS, classic scripts on a `window.SA` namespace, no build step (same approach as Loyal Paws). Sound effects are synthesized with WebAudio. The canvas has a logical height of 720, and its width follows the screen (960–1600). Saves go to `localStorage`. The planned path to iOS is a Capacitor wrapper.

## The whole Solar System and the Grand Tour (2026-10-13)
All 8 planets are now playable. Unlock order: Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Venus → Mercury → Uranus → Neptune.
- **Mercury: Sunrise Escape.** Drag Cosmo to stay in the comfy twilight band between the 430 °C day and the −180 °C night. The band drifts, and the walk (and its 5 discoveries) only moves forward while Cosmo is comfy. ## Weak-spot pass (2026-10-13)
A fresh-eyes audit of the newest features, playing as a 6-year-old, a 9-year-old and a parent, plus a code review of `base.js`, `critters.js`, `belt.js` and `sun.js`. Facts in the new missions were rechecked (Ceres salt spots, Vesta's mountain, Ida and Dactyl, Psyche, the Sun's life stages, moon counts); none needed changes. The goal was to fix rather than add.
- **Critters only peek during play.** They used to appear over the intro, chapter cheers in Star Story, Earth's "friend is happy" moment, the famous-asteroid visit, Saturn's moon stops and the Mission Complete banner. A critter already peeking now slides away when such a moment starts (`QUIET` phases in `critters.js`).
- **Critters no longer hide under Cosmo's speech bubble.** On phones, left-side peeks in Mercury and the asteroid belt sat behind the bubble (Mercury's "Too hot!" tips are frequent). Left-side peeks now stay in the upper half. The Moon critter peeks below the "Seen from Earth" window instead of over it.
- **Asteroid belt drop bug.** The pods' drop zones overlap, and the last matching pod won. In Little Explorer mode, a rock dropped on the bottom of its own pod counted as the pod below and boinged back as "wrong". The nearest pod now wins. The pods' sample rocks are also bigger, so non-readers can match by look.
- **Sticker tray for non-readers.** Opening the tray now says (aloud and in a toast) how to use it, once per visit; its how-to line was small print and hidden on phones. Stickers the child already has come first, and all locked ones move to a final "🔒 Still to find" group. Before, a new player's few usable stickers were scrolled off-screen behind rows of padlocks.
- **Space Base speed.** Planet stickers are drawn once into a small cached picture instead of being fully re-shaded each frame. With 40 stickers, the base drew about 4× faster in testing (5.4 ms down to 1.4 ms per frame on a desktop), which older iPads will feel most.
- **Sticker saves.** The screen-only "pop" animation is no longer saved. Leaving the base mid-drag saves the sticker's place. On load, stickers that can't be drawn (an unknown key would have been invisible but still grabbable), off-screen positions, odd sizes and more than 40 stickers are repaired.
- **Critter Club** (tap 🐾 at the Space Base): all 11 critters, in colour with their name and line once found, or as silhouettes with the place they hide ("Watch the edges of the screen!"). Everything is read aloud. Finding the last critter unlocks a 🏅 **Critter Club medal** sticker (`SA.DECOR`, appended so saved sticker keys stay valid).
- **Little Explorer:** critter peeks last 10 s instead of 7.5 s. **Calm effects:** the twinkles around a peeking critter glow steadily instead of blinking.
- **App Store screenshots refreshed.** `tools/screenshots.html` now includes the Space Base, a critter peeking (Tilt at Uranus), Star Story's planetary nebula and the asteroid belt's tractor beam. It renders Apple's maximum of 10 per device (`?all=1` adds Venus, Mercury, Uranus and Neptune). The order and captions are in `APP_STORE_LISTING.md`.
